<Section address="0x01" title="The study">
  <Tabs items={[{ id: 'problema', label: 'The problem' }, { id: 'metodos', label: 'The methods', count: 4 }, { id: 'resultado', label: 'The result' }]}>
    <TabPanel id="problema">
      <div class="split">
        <p class="statement">The manufacturer's power curve describes a single turbine under ideal conditions. A real wind farm doesn't behave like that.</p>
        <div class="split-side">
          <p>Projecting generation runs into two obstacles: long on-site wind series are rarely available, and converting wind into power is uncertain. Within the same speed range, power varies by hundreds of kilowatts.</p>
          <p>The non-parametric methods that handle this uncertainty are more accurate, but hard to implement. WindAnalyzer turns two published methodologies into an interactive decision-support tool.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </TabPanel>
    <TabPanel id="metodos">
      <div class="auto-grid">
        <Card id="M/01" title="Wind series" flat><p>MERRA-2 reanalysis for any point in Brazil, extrapolated to hub height and corrected by the ratio between the means of the series and of an INMET station.</p></Card>
        <Card id="M/02" title="K-means" flat><p>Splits the series into wind regimes. The number of clusters is picked automatically, testing K from 1 to 30 with the elbow method.</p></Card>
        <Card id="M/03" title="KDE" flat><p>Estimates the power distribution in each regime straight from the data, with a Gaussian kernel, without imposing a distribution family.</p></Card>
        <Card id="M/04" title="Monte Carlo" flat><p>Assigns each projected speed to its regime and samples power from the estimated densities, generating generation scenarios.</p></Card>
      </div>
    </TabPanel>
    <TabPanel id="resultado">
      <div class="auto-grid">
        <Stat label="Confidence band" value="90%" note="5th and 95th percentiles of the scenarios" />
        <Stat label="Clusters in the demo" value="9" note="chosen with the elbow method" />
        <Stat label="Stratification strategies" value="4" note="single · monthly · hourly · month-hour" />
      </div>
      <p class="max-prose">In the demo case, with hourly series from 2013 and 2014 and wind projections for 2025, the estimated generation reproduced the central tendency and spread of the historical series. The estimated densities came out unimodal, bimodal and skewed, which no single distribution family could reproduce at once.</p>
